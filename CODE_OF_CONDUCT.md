# Code Of Conduct

OpenAEO follows the Contributor Covenant principles: be respectful, constructive, and focused on making the project useful for publishers, researchers, and developers.

Harassment, abuse, discrimination, and bad-faith participation are not welcome.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors that undermine a safe and productive project environment.

OpenAEO is especially strict about bad-faith SEO behavior. Contributions that promote spam, cloaking, prompt injection, or source misattribution may be closed even when they are technically functional.
